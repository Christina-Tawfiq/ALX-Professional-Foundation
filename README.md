# ALX Professional Foundation

This repository documents my completion of the **ALX Professional Foundation** program, delivered in partnership with the Mastercard Foundation.  
The program provided 8 weeks of engaging learning content, hands-on deliverables, and collaborative experiences designed to build essential professional skills for success in tech careers.

---

## 📌 Program Overview
The Professional Foundation program equips learners with the fundamental skills needed to thrive in the workplace and the tech industry.  
Key highlights include:
- Career readiness and professional development  
- Collaborative learning in squads and peer networks  
- Practical application through real-world exercises and guided deliverables  

---

## 🧩 Meta Skills
The program focuses on 8 meta-skills that prepare learners to succeed in any workplace:
1. **Leading Self** – Self-awareness, personal values, emotional intelligence, growth mindset, healthy habits  
2. **Leading Others** – Empathy, collaboration, feedback, relationship building, diversity  
3. **Communicating for Impact** – Audience awareness, writing, storytelling, presentation skills  
4. **Quantitative Reasoning** – Data contextualisation, empirical research, decision making, problem solving  
5. **Entrepreneurial Thinking** – Opportunity identification, creative solutions, design thinking, innovation  
6. **Critical Thinking** – Authentic inquiry, research analysis, synthesis, judgement  
7. **Managing Complex Tasks** – Project scoping, structuring, planning, coordination, execution  
8. **Tech Skills** – Google Workspace, AI tools, product team roles, ethics in tech  

---

## 📂 Milestones
- **Milestone 1 – Skills Map Baseline** → Initial self-assessment of strengths, growth areas, and career goals.  
- **Milestone 2 – Personal Mission Statement** → Defined personal mission, values, and growth mindset.  
- **Milestone 3 – Problem Statement & Web Research** → Framed a problem statement, asked effective questions, conducted ethical research.  
- **Milestone 4 – Presentation Report** → Created a presentation applying communication for impact and storytelling.  
- **Milestone 5 – Team Problem Statement & Data Visualization** → Collaborated with a team to define a problem, clean data, and visualize findings.  
- **Milestone 6 – Wireframe & User Interview Report** → Conducted user interviews, built personas, created initial wireframes.  
- **Milestone 7 – Final Prototype & Pitch Deck** → Developed a prototype, prepared a pitch deck, and presented solution recommendations.  
- **Milestone 8 – Professional Kit** → Built a resume, LinkedIn profile, elevator pitch, and professional bio.

## 🗓️ Weekly Flow
- **Week 1 – Getting Started** → Onboarding, values & community, skills baseline.  
- **Week 2 – Self Awareness & Growth Mindset** → Mission statement, empathy, emotional intelligence.  
- **Week 3 – Problem Definition & Research** → Habit formation, problem statements, web research.  
- **Week 4 – Communication for Impact** → Presentations, technical writing, project scoping.  
- **Week 5 – Teamwork & Data Skills** → Agile roles, teamwork, data gathering, visualization.  
- **Week 6 – Human-Centered Design** → User interviews, personas, wireframes, collaboration.  
- **Week 7 – Prototyping & Pitching** → Solution generation, product requirements, prototype, pitch deck.  
- **Week 8 – Career Readiness** → Resume, LinkedIn, elevator pitch, professional bio, interview skills.
